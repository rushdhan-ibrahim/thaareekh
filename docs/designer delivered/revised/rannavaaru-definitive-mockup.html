uhammad Thakurufaanu al-A'lam is the national hero of the Maldives, revered for leading the liberation of the islands from Portuguese occupation. Born in Utheem, Thiladhummathi Atoll, he organised a guerrilla campaign from the northern atolls that culminated in the overthrow of the Portuguese garrison in Malé in 1573.

His reign marked the founding of the Utheemu dynasty and a new era of sovereignty. He is credited with introducing Thaana script, reorganising the military, and establishing systems that endured for generations.
