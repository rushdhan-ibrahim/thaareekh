Cabinet of Ink & Light

remastered studies, volumes, and the expanded artefact library

I studied the three original pages and kept what was already strong: the paper, the codex voice, the code-underlay plates, and the sense that geometry is being treated as liturgy.

Then I added a shared movement library, a page enhancer for corners, frames, dormant-motion control, an index for navigating the cabinet, and a new volume of twelve more talismanic artefacts.
